MUNICIPAL DEPARTMENTS

Services & Grievance Categories

CivicCare connects citizens directly to responsible department teams for fast resolution.

Potholes

Report hazardous road craters, broken pavement, asphalt damage, and dangerous street surface defects.

Waste Disposal

Report overflowing garbage dumps, uncollected residential waste, illegal trash dumping, and foul odor hazards.

Streetlights

Report non-functional streetlights, dark lanes, broken light poles, fused LED fixtures, and exposed wiring.

Water Leakage

Report main supply pipeline bursts, drinking water wastage, valve leaks, and low water pressure issues.

Drainage

Report clogged stormwater gutters, monsoon street waterlogging, sewage line blockages, and open manholes.

Other Civic Issues

Report public park damages, stray animal concerns, damaged signage, or general public infrastructure defects.

TRANSPARENT PROCESS

How CivicCare Works

Four simple steps to get public grievances resolved by municipal field teams.

1
Report

Snap a photo, write a brief description, and pin the exact GPS location of the grievance on the map.

2
Assign

The smart routing engine dispatches your complaint to the concerned department and assigned field officer.

3
Resolve

Field repair teams conduct on-site repairs and upload photo evidence of completed work to the case file.

4
Feedback

Citizens inspect the resolution evidence photo, verify repair quality, and rate service satisfaction.

ABOUT CIVICCARE

Empowering Citizens with Smart Urban Governance

CivicCare is a modern public grievance management system designed to streamline municipal operations and bridge the gap between citizens and municipal authorities.

By leveraging GPS location mapping, photo evidence verification, role-based workflows, and live SLA tracking, CivicCare ensures every reported issue is addressed with accountability and speed.

Privacy Policy
1. Data Collection

CivicCare collects personal information such as name, email, phone number, location coordinates, and photo evidence solely for processing and resolving public grievances.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used to route complaints to municipal officers, contact you for clarification, and record service metrics. Personal details are never sold to third parties.

3. Security

Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Access to citizen grievances is protected via JWT token authentication.

Terms of Service
1. Reporting Guidelines

Citizens must submit genuine civic grievance information. False, offensive, or fraudulent reporting may lead to account suspension.

2. Resolution SLAs

Municipal departments strive to resolve grievances according to target service level agreements based on priority (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
